## Description (4000)

Astra Dial turns the volume knob on your keyboard into a mode wheel.

Double-press the knob to open the wheel, turn to choose a mode, and press to switch. Long-press any time to go back to Volume.

MODES
• Volume: the knob works as it always has
• Scroll: smooth scrolling under the pointer
• Zoom: zoom in and out (⌘+ / ⌘−), press for actual size
• Apps: flip through open apps like ⌘Tab
• Tabs: next and previous tab in your browser or editor
• Desktops: move between Spaces, press for Mission Control
• Undo: turn back to undo, forward to redo, press to save
• Scrub: step through video, press to play or pause
• Slides: next and previous slide in Keynote or PowerPoint
• Custom 1–3: name them and record any shortcuts for turn and press

PER-APP MODES
Give an app its own mode and Astra Dial switches to it when the app comes to the front: Slides for Keynote, Scrub for your video player, a Custom mode with your editor's shortcuts.

MAKE IT YOURS
Reorder and hide modes, choose what a press does in each, turn on spin acceleration per mode (spin faster to move further), and tune scroll speed and timings.

WORKS WITH YOUR KEYBOARD
Astra Dial works with keyboards whose knob sends volume keys, as the knobs on Keychron and most other mechanical keyboards do. Pair your keyboard by turning its knob; other keyboards' volume keys keep working normally.

PRIVATE
No accounts, no analytics, no network connections. Astra Dial needs Input Monitoring to read the knob and Accessibility to perform scrolls and shortcuts; it reads only the knob, and nothing leaves your Mac.

## App Review notes

Astra Dial needs a keyboard with a volume knob, which the reviewer may not have. A demo video
is attached: <link>.

To try it without a knob, a keyboard with dedicated volume keys works the same way (most USB
media keyboards; check before submitting whether a Mac's built-in F11/F12 do too):

1. Launch Astra Dial; it lives in the menu bar.
2. Click the menu bar icon and grant Input Monitoring, then Accessibility, from the buttons
   there. Quit and reopen if prompted.
3. Click Pair… and press Volume Up once. That keyboard is now paired.
4. Choose Scroll from the Mode menu in the menu bar window, then press Volume Up/Down over a
   long web page: the page scrolls and the volume doesn't change.
5. Pressing Mute twice quickly opens the mode wheel; Volume Up/Down moves the highlight and
   Mute selects.

Why the permissions: Input Monitoring reads the knob's volume keys from the paired keyboard
only. Accessibility lets Astra Dial stop those keys changing the volume while another mode is
active, and post the scroll and keyboard events the active mode performs. Astra Dial does not
read other input, inspect other apps' content, or use the network.
