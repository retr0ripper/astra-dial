# Astra Dial privacy policy

_Last updated: 24 October 2026_

Astra Dial collects no personal data. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking,
and the Mac App Store version makes no network connections at all.

**What it reads.** With your permission (Input Monitoring), Astra Dial reads the volume knob,
which arrives as volume and mute keys, from the keyboard you pair. It reads nothing else you
type, and nothing it reads leaves your Mac or is stored.

**What it does with Accessibility.** With your permission, Astra Dial stops the knob's volume
keys reaching macOS while you use another mode, and sends the scrolls and keyboard shortcuts
that mode performs, such as ⌃Tab in Tabs mode, to the app in front.

**What it stores.** Your settings (the modes you use, recorded shortcuts, app profiles, and the
paired keyboard's name and USB vendor and product IDs) are kept on your Mac in the app's own preferences.
Deleting the app deletes them.

**Versions outside the App Store** (GitHub and Homebrew) check
`api.github.com/repos/retr0ripper/astra-dial/releases/latest` once a day for a newer release;
you can turn that off in Settings. GitHub's own privacy policy covers that request.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/retr0ripper/astra-dial/issues.
